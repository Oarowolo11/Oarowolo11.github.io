I was a project supervisor for the course Interdisciplinary Advanced Artificial Intelligence Project (IAAIP CS520200). IAAIP CS520200 is a Master level course for computer science students at the Electrical Engineering, Mathematics and Computer Science faculty. Students carried out research projects applying artificial intelligence to climate-related problems in various departments. I supervised a team of 4 students who researched the use of graph neural networks for power flow analysis.
