I was a teaching assistant for the Machine Learning Workflows for Digital Energy Systems course (SET 3125) at TU Delft. SET 3125 is a Master level course for the Sustainable Energy Technology Master program at Electrical Engineering, Mathematics and Computer Science Faculty. The course introduces students to statistical learning concepts, supervised regression, and training neural networks. The students also learn designing machine learning workflows, physics informed learning for energy applications and learning with inductive biases in energy grids. I did the following:

- Supported the instructor with teaching material preparation and delivery
- Graded student assignments, exams and projects
- Conducted office hours for students to answer questions
