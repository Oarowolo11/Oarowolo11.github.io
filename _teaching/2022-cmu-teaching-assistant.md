I was a teaching assistant for the Photovoltaic Systems Engineering course (ECE 18-883) at Carnegie Mellon University. ECE 18-883 is a Master level course for the Electrical and Computer Science program at the College of Engineering. The course introduces students to assessment of solar resources, analysis of solar panel rating, output and efficiency, and the design of off-grid PV systems. I did the following:

- Supported the instructor with teaching material preparation and delivery
- Graded student assignments, exams and projects
- Conducted office hours for students to answer questions
