2025 was a productive year for my PhD research. Our work on [Spatio-Temporal Graph Neural Networks for Multi-Period Optimal Power Flow](https://doi.org/10.1109/ISGTEurope64741.2025.11305408) was presented at IEEE PES ISGT Europe 2025, and two journal papers appeared: [Learning-Accelerated ADMM for Stochastic Power System Scheduling](https://doi.org/10.1109/TSTE.2025.3562640) in *IEEE Transactions on Sustainable Energy* and [Exploring extrapolation of machine learning models for power system time domain simulation](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.segan.2025.101908) in *Sustainable Energy, Grids and Networks*.
